# The Hidden Factors Behind Young Adult Loan Defaults

For many young adults, taking out a loan is one of the first major financial decisions they will make. Having a clear understanding of why certain are more likely to default can help both lenders and borrowers make more informed decisions before financial problems begin


### Problem Statement
In this project, the specific problem statement is predicting load default risk of young adults within the first year of the loan being issued. Loan defaults are significant challenges for financial institutions, especially as it can lead to economic loss for the company and also affect borrowers’ long-term credit standing. Young adults are often in a transitional phase, facing new financial responsibilities while having limited experience managing debt, which increases their likelihood of early repayment difficulties. Current predictive models tend to consider the broader population, potentially overlooking patterns specific to younger borrowers and the critical early stages of a loan. By concentrating on the first year after a loan is issued for this demographic, this project aims to identify early warning signs that indicate a higher likelihood of default, allowing for more targeted strategies to mitigate risk and have better outcomes for both the financial institutions and their clients.

### Solution Description
By creating a predictive machine learning model and examining the feature importance for that model, the goal is to provide a better understanding of loan defaults for the young adult demographic. Examining the feature importance that a predictive model outputs is helpful to both the financial institution and the client for understanding how the loan default risks have been calculated.
